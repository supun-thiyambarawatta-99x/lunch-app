# Personas

## Lunch Coordinator

**Role**: The internal office staff member responsible for running the shared lunch process.

**Goals**:

- Maintain an accurate current roster.
- Complete attendance, allocation, ordering, and charging without manual calculations.
- Identify paid and unpaid charges and each person's outstanding balance.
- Correct records while retaining an auditable daily history.

**Characteristics**:

- Uses the internal web application on a desktop browser.
- Is the only authenticated system user in the first release.
- Needs clear daily summaries because the process repeats frequently.

## Office Attendee

**Role**: A person listed on the office roster who either brings food from home or shares the purchased-lunch cost on a given day.

**Goals**:

- Be represented accurately in daily attendance and cost records.
- Have a correct outstanding balance.

**Characteristics**:

- Does not log in or interact directly with the first-release application.
- Is managed by the Lunch Coordinator.
